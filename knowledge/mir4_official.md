## 1. What makes a character strong (and what transfers to a buyer)
Everything sealed with an NFT character transfers to the buyer (NFT Character, F527):
- Level, Power Score, all ability stats, levels of all 13 skills.
- Equipped gear with grade, tier, enhance level, up to 8 additional properties, Primal Force stats, Void Resonance slots: Weapon, Secondary Weapon, Top/Bottoms/Gloves/Shoes, Necklace/Bracelet/Ring/Earrings, Dragon Artifact, Ancient Treasure. All owned gear too.
- Spirits (deployed and owned, incl. Spirit Treasures), Magical Soul Orbs, Magic Stones, Mystical Pieces, Transference Equipment.
- Training: Constitution, Inner Force (Muscle Strength, Nine Yin, Nine Yang, Violet Mist, Northern Profound, Toad Stance), Potential, Solitude Training.
- Codex (Character / Reputation / Server / Time Limited / Collection), Conquest stage, Mystique, Cosmic Harmony, inventory, held resources (Copper, Energy, Darksteel, Dragon Jade, Speed-ups, Ancient Coin). Gold does NOT transfer.
Caveats when loading (F527):
- Server, Reputation and Time-Limited Codex merge with the buyer's account codex; EXPIRED time-limited codex effects are not applied. A listing's codex % can overstate lasting power.
- Character can only be loaded on the server it was sealed in, and you need 1 empty character slot.
- Karma lists are deleted; daily Portal/Raid/mission counts reset; no Septaria from Sanctuary of HYDRA on load day.
- Mythic (awakened) gear is non-tradable as an item but stays on the character (F2028).

## 3. Classes (official descriptions, mir4global.com)
- Warrior: greatsword melee, sturdy armor, front-line. Ultimate Dragon Flame.
- Sorcerer: elemental magic, strong solo, stronger when protected. Ultimate Dragon Tornado.
- Taoist: sword + spells, divine recovery (support/heal). Ultimate Ray of Light.
- Lancer: spear, attacks and defends at once, breaks formations. Ultimate Dragon Spear.
- Arbalist: fast-firing arbalest, tracking and escape, arrow rain. Ultimate Arrow Rain.
- Darkist: ranged poison and curses. Ultimate Asura.
- Spirit Summoner: new 8th class added Aug 18, 2026 (F2598): ranged spell class, wide MP control and buffs. Not yet seen in NFT listings we track.
The official site does not publish a class tier list; "strongest class" claims are not official.

## 4. What drives price (official mechanics + our data)
- Power Score is the official headline stat and the main price driver (our 240 sales: price ~ power^6.7).
- Things that are expensive to rebuild: Dragon Forge stages (low success, 10M Darksteel per try), Mythic awakening, Legendary/Mythic spirits, Legendary Transference +8 or higher, Magic Stone special enhance Lv 7.
- Things that do not carry full value to the buyer: expired time-limited codex, Gold (not sealed).
- Fees: WEMIX PLAY Marketplace takes 5% commission on completed trades, plus WEMIX gas per transaction (F1869, xdraco.com/nft/playbook). Unsold listings delist after 7 days. Minimum price 0.1 WEMIX.
- Prices are set in WEMIX, so USD prices move with the WEMIX token.

## 5. NFT / XDRACO trade rules
- Mint: Lv 60+ and 100,000+ Power Score, 4,000 Gold; account linked to Google/Apple/Facebook and WEMIX PLAY (F527).
- Seal: 1,000 Gold, character in Bicheon Castle, alignment > 0, not in a clan or party, no market items, no 5-day Wayfarer/NFT cooldown, not during siege. Sealed character is unplayable (F527).
- Marketplace: Mainnet NFT required; English auction with 3-hour default bidding window after highest bid; a bid cannot be cancelled once made; listing goes live 3 minutes after registering (F1869).
- Item sealing to XDRACO costs Rare 100 / Epic 500 / Legendary 2,000 / Mythic 10,000 Gold (F394).

## 8. Buyer checklist (applied in the site's Guide tab)
- Check server: you can only play it on the server it was sealed in, with a free slot.
- Discount codex % that comes from time-limited codex.
- Look at Dragon Forge stages and Mythic gear: the costliest growth to rebuild.
- Count spirit slots (Lv 175+ for slot 9, 195 for slot 10) and Legendary/Mythic spirits.
- 5% marketplace fee is paid by the seller; gas is extra; bids cannot be cancelled.
