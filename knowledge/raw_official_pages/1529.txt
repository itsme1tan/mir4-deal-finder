Zoetrope
Dragonians can watch advertisements through ‘Zoetrope’ and can obtain ‘Horse Emblem’ and ‘Zoetrope Reward Box’ which can grant Dragonians a variety of rewards.
Precautions
Zoetrope
Precautions
1) Advertisements are not available on the PC and HUAWEI platform.
2) Advertisements are not available while using Expedition and Domination Server content.
3) A separate data bill could be charged if watching an advertisement while connected to a Cellular Network, (Wi-Fi Connection Recommended)
4) Gameplay will be available once the advertisement is over.
5) Obtainable Horse Emblem amount and View Count for the Reward Box will be reset every day at 00:00(UTC+8)
Zoetrope
You can watch advertisements through [Shop - Zoetrope - View] and can obtain ‘Horse Emblem’ that can be exchanged for rewards.
1. Using the Zoetrope
Click on: [Shop - Zoetrope - View] to view Zoetrope advertisements.
① You can check the amount of Horse Emblems that are obtainable and obtain Horse Emblems by clicking on the ‘Obtain Emblem’ Button.
② Accumulate more View Count by watching advertisements in Zoetrope to obtain the ‘Zoetrope Reward Box’.
2. Exchange Station
① Click on: [Zoetrope - Exchange Station] to exchange ‘Horse Emblems’ for a variety of rewards.
② You can check the amount of ‘Horse Emblem’ needed to exchange a product and exchange ‘Horse Emblems’ for rewards.
3. Details
1) ‘Horse Emblems’ are Character-bound and cannot be shared between accounts or servers.
2) Items purchased through: [Zoetrope - Exchange Station] cannot be traded.
3) Watching 1 advertisement rewards you with 1 ‘Horse Emblem’.
4) If you reach the daily capacity of obtainable ‘Horse Emblem’, you can continue to watch more advertisements, but you will not be able to acquire more ‘Horse Emblems’.
5) ‘Zoetrope Reward Box’ View Count’s daily capacity is 10 times, and is accumulated every time you obtain 1 ‘Horse Emblem’.
6) Accumulated Reward Box View Count gets reset daily at 00:00(UTC+8).
