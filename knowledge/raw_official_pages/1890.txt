Divine Dragon Descent
Divine Dragon Descent allows you to unlock your Pressure Points using Dragon Materials, drawing out hidden potential and enabling extraordinary growth.

Divine Dragon Descent
- Divine Dragon Descent is unlocked when the character reaches Level 110.
- Access the feature by navigating to the Conquest > Divine Dragon Descent tab in the menu.
① Divine Dragon Descent consists of three tabs:
- Divine Dragon Descent: Check the stats and unlock status of each Pressure Point.
- Unlock Divine Dragon Descent: Unlock or re-unlock Pressure Points.
- Yearn Divine Dragon Descent: Adjust the stats granted to Pressure Points.
② Displays the Power Score gained through Divine Dragon Descent, with detailed stats available by clicking the magnifying glass icon on the right.
③ Divine Dragon Descent consists of six Pressure Points, each unlocked with different Dragon Materials:

④ Check the unlock status and stats of the selected Pressure Point.
- For locked Pressure Points, view the material items required for unlocking.
- For unlocked Pressure Points, review the currently applied stats.
⑤ Use the buttons at the bottom right of the screen to navigate directly to the Unlock Divine Dragon Descent or Yearn tab.
Unlock Divine Dragon Descent
- Unlocking a Pressure Point grants multiple stats, with higher-grade materials yielding more stats.
① Select a Pressure Point to unlock or re-unlock from the right side of the screen.
② Dragon Materials and Dragonsteel are consumed when unlocking or re-unlocking Pressure Points.
- The ‘Divine Dragon’s Key’ item can also be used for unlocking or re-unlocking Pressure Points.

- The number of stats granted when unlocking or re-unlocking depends on the grade.
③ Review the stats applied to the selected Pressure Point and the stats that can be obtained.
④ After re-unlocking, select which stats to apply.
- Click the [Apply] button to apply the ‘Changed Unlocked Stats.’
- Click the [Cancel] button to keep the "Previous Unlocked Stats."
※ If you proceed with Wayfarer’s Travel before confirming the re-unlocked Pressure Point stats, the ‘Changed Unlocked Stats’ will not be saved and will disappear.
Yearn Divine Dragon Descent
- Yearn allows you to adjust the stats of an unlocked Pressure Point.
① Yearn uses Pledge Scroll of Yearning, Dragonsteel, and Copper.
- The more stats assigned to a Pressure Point, the more materials are required.
- Pledge Scroll of Yearning can be obtained through Disenchant.
② Check the types of stats available for the selected Pressure Point.
- There are 20 types of stats for each Pressure Point, and when Yearn occurs, the types and grades of all stats are readjusted at once.
- The highest stat grade achievable through Yearn is SS-grade.
③ When you proceed with Yearn,  you can compare the current stats with the new stats on the right side of the screen.
- Click the [Cancel] button to keep the ‘Current Stats.’
- Click the [Apply] button to apply the "New Stats."
※ If you proceed with Wayfarer’s Travel before confirming the stats of an Yearn, the ‘New Stats’ will not be saved and will disappear.
