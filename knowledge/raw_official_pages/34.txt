Enhance
- Tap Forge > Enhance tab in the Menu.
① You can select weapons, armor, or accessories to enhance.
② To enhance an equipment, you need an Enhancement Stone of the same grade as the equipment.
There are Safe Intervals and Danger Zones in enhancing, and equipment is destroyed upon failure in a Danger Zone.
③ Tapping the Craft button opens Craft > Crafting window.
④ Auto-enhance enables enhancing an Uncommon item to a selected enhancement level in one go.
* Up to 12 items of the same grade can be registered by clicking the 'Multi-Register' upon Auto-Enhance, and you cannot use the feature for items in the following specifics: (Dragon Artifact, Max Enhancement Stage, Locked Status)
Enchant
- Tap Forge > Enchant tab in the Menu.
① You can use Enchant Scrolls to enchant the options of weapons, armor, or accessories.
② Tap the magnifying glass icon to view the stats of an enchanted item.
③ Tap the Enchantment Info button to enchant an item and check the stats that can be gained.
④ Tap the Enchant button to enchant options.
- Enchant will be separated into Normal and Special Enchant.
- Upon Special Enchant, you can obtain 1 additional Enchant property, and the obtainable Max grade will be superior compared to Normal Enchant.
※ Regarding Cost in Special Enchant, Dragonsteel will be consumed instead of Copper.
※ Items with unidentified options will not be registerable to Market.
Special Enhancement
A special enhancement system that allows you to increase the stats of Epic-grade or higher Magic Stones, Spectrumites, Spirit Treasures, and Mystical Pieces. Special Enhancement consumes various enhancement materials and can be strengthened up to Stage 7.
[Special Enhancement Notes]
1. Special Enhancement uses grade-appropriate ‘Special Enhancement Stones’, Darksteel, and Dragonsteel.
- You can also use the grade-appropriate ‘Oil of Blessing’ to increase the enhancement success rate by 10%.
2. On Enhancement Great Failure, the item will be destroyed.
- If destroyed, you may acquire items such as Treasure Shard, Spacetime Powder, Noirsoul Herb, or Ice Crystal at a set probability.
3. Only Epic-grade or higher Magic Stones, Spectrumites, Spirit Treasures, and Mystical Pieces can undergo Special Enhancement.
- Magic Stones, Spectrumites, Spirit Treasures: Epic, Legendary, Mythic grades
- Mystical Piece: Epic, Legendary grades
[Special Enhancement Process]
① You can check your owned Magic Stones, Spectrumites, Spirit Treasures, and Mystical Piece items through the following paths.
- Magic Stones, Spectrumites: Menu > Character > Magic Stone
- Spirit Treasures: Menu > Spirit
- Mystical Piece: Menu > Character > Mystical Piece
※ You can also access the Special Enhancement function via Menu > Forge > Special Enhancement.
② The list of items eligible for Special Enhancement can be viewed on the right side of the tab.
③ After registering the target item, you can preview the stat increases gained upon success on the bottom left.
④ Check the success rate and ‘Danger Zone’ information.
⑤ Click the ‘i’ icon next to the ‘Danger Zone’ to review important notes for Special Enhancement.
Dragon Forge
- Dragon Forge is an exclusive reinforcement for the growth of Legendary IV Tier items.
- You can move to Dragon Forge windows from Menu > Forge > Dragon Forge or the Dragon Forge tab located on the left of the information window for Legendary IV Tier equipment
① Register the targeted equipment for Dragon Forge at the right of your in-game window.
- Dragon Forge is only available for Mythic grade or Tier 4 Legendary grade equipment (Weapon/Secondary Weapon/Armor/Accessory).
② Required materials and additional stats upon Dragon Forge Success can be viewed.
- Legendary Dragon Material, Dragon Sphere, White Dragon Essence, and that of Black Dragon will be consumed per try.
- There are seven stages in total, and the required number of materials increases with each stage.
- Success Chance for Dragon Forge varies per stage, and the targeted equipment will not be destroyed upon resulting in Failure.

Primal Force
- Primal Force unlocks when your character reaches Level 120. This system allows you to enhance equipment with additional stats, enabling even greater character growth.
- You can access Primal Force through Forge > Primal Force, or by selecting the Primal Force tab in the equipment details window of Epic/Legendary/Mythic items that contain Resonance.
① You can register the equipment to perform Primal Force on the right side of the screen.
- Primal Force can be performed on Epic grade or higher equipment that has Resonance applied.
- When acquiring Rare-grade or higher equipment through crafting, drops, random boxes, Awakening, etc., there is a chance to obtain equipment with Resonance.
※ When crafting Mythic equipment through Awakening, Void Resonance Slots and Primal Force options from the original Legendary item will NOT transfer.
- Each equipment can have up to 4 Void Resonance Slots, and equipment with more slots can receive more powerful enhancements through Primal Force.
- Equipment with Resonance appears across a total of 14 equipment slots (Weapon/Secondary Weapon/5 Armor slots/7 Accessory slots).
② You can check the registered equipment and materials used for Primal Force.
- Primal Force uses Epic-grade or higher Magic Stones, Dragon Steel, and Darksteel.
③ You can select the Magic Stone to register as Primal Force material on the right side of the screen.
- The type of Magic Stone required varies by equipment slot. Using higher-grade or higher-tier Magic Stones grants higher stat values.

Infuse
- Infuse unlocks when your character reaches Level 120 and allows transferring Void Resonance Slots from one piece of equipment to another of a higher grade.
- Characters below Level 120 may use Rare–Legendary equipment (with 4 Void Resonance Slots) as Infuse materials.
- Access via Forge > Primal Force > Infuse.
① You can register the equipment that will receive Resonance and the equipment that will provide Resonance on the right side of the screen. The equipment types are Weapon / Armor / Accessory / Secondary Weapon, and Infusion is only possible when the types of the receiving and providing equipment are the same.
② You can check the equipment registered for Infuse.
- Infuse uses the equipment to be infused, the material equipment, Divine Anvil, and Copper.
③ You can check the success rate of Infuse.
- The higher the tier of the providing equipment (right side), the higher the success rate becomes.

Acquisition Path for the "Divine Anvil" Infuse Material
- The Divine Anvil used in Infuse can be obtained through NPC crafting/exchange, defeating monsters in Redmoon Purgatory, Raid, and Boss Raid.
- In each major region's city, you can craft Divine Anvils through the <Craftsman> NPC.
- When crafting, there is a 5% chance for a Great Success, and if a Great Success occurs, you can obtain a tradeable Divine Anvil.
