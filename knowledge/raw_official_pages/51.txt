Private Warehouse
A service that allows you to store the items in your bag in a warehouse in the village.
Warehouse
Warehouse
Your character's Private Warehouse can be accessed through merchant NPC 'Mukseon' found in every village.
You can restore equipment, materials, Magic Stones, Spirits, and other miscellaneous items regardless of their tradability, but event items cannot be stored.
