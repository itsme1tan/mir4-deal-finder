Force Map
The Force Map is a system that allows you to view the ruling power structure across the entire Mir continent at a glance.
Force Map Information
Changing Force Colors
Force Map Effects
Force Map Information
You can access the Force Map by tapping [Menu > War > Force Map].
1. Through the Force Map, you can view the force structure by Region and Server at a glance.
2. By clicking on a Region, you can check the occupation status of Sabuk Castle and Bicheon Castle in that Region.
Changing Force Colors
Castellan of Sabuk Castle and Bicheon Castle can change the color of their force on the Force Map.
① Go to [Menu > War > Bicheon Castle or Sabuk Castle].
② Tap the Force Color Change function.
③ Select your desired color and apply the change.
Force Map Effects
- For the server you belong to, a sparkling effect will be displayed.
- For servers where Bicheon Castle bidding competition is in progress, a red circle effect will be displayed.
