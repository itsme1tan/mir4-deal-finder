Signing in
① You can play Mir 4 with a Google, Apple, Facebook, or guest account.
② As some contents in Mir 4 may not be accessible with a guest account, it is recommended to link a social account.
Linking an account
- You can link a single Mir 4 game account with accounts from multiple platforms. (E.g. After creating an account using an Apple account, you can also link it with a Facebook account)
Brazil Age Verification (KWS)
- If you access MIR4 from Brazil, the KWS age verification process will begin after login.
- The KWS system Verification can be completed through email.
1) Click the [Send] button to send a verification message to the email address that has been input.
2) Check your Email inbox to complete the verification, then click the [Verification Complete] button in-game to access the game immediately.
⚠️ Game access will be restricted if verification fails or is not completed, in accordance with Brazilian laws protecting the rights of children and adolescents.
