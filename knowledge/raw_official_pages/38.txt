Conquest
Meeting the promotion requirements of various buildings in Conquest allows you to upgrade the buildings and gain a range of achievement effects. Leveling up Conquest is particularly important as it removes the level cap of your character.
Conquest
Conquest Stage Completion Effect
Conquest
- Tap the Conquest tab in the Menu to open the Conquest window.
- A total of 10 buildings can be upgraded.
- Tapping each building shows whether its upgrade requirements have been met and the Power Score increased by the building.
- Tap the i button next to the building's level to view the effects you can get at each level.
※ Wayfarer Travel and NFT Character Sealing is unavailable when progressing with Conquest Promotion
