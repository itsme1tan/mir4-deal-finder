Arena Basic Information
1. The Arena is available to characters who have reached Level 80 or higher and 100,000 Power Score or higher.
2. The Arena is divided into two teams. Each team must have at least one participant to proceed. Up to 5 players per team can participate, with a maximum of 10 players total.
3. No death penalties are applied while participating in the Arena.
4. The Arena can be entered up to 10 times per day, and entry counts are reset daily at 16:00 (UTC) based on each server region’s local time.
- ASIA (UTC+8): 12:00 am
- INMENA (UTC+6): 10:00 pm
- EU (UTC+2): 06:00 pm
- SA (UTC-3): 01:00 pm
- NA (UTC-4): 12:00 pm
※ Arena entry counts are consumed at 1 entry per match, regardless of the selected mode (Single Match or Best of 3).
Arena Party Creation
- You can access the Arena screen through [Menu > War > Arena].
① You can check the Arena entry requirements and available play time, then select the match mode (Single Match / Best of 3).
② You can view your remaining Arena entry count.
③ Press the [Find Arena] button to join an existing Arena party.
④ Press the [Create Arena Party] button to set the conditions for a new Arena party.
⑤ You can configure the party description, visibility settings, and Power Score requirement for the Arena party you are creating.
- Public Party: Players can freely join the party without a password.
- Private Party: A password can be set, and only users who enter the correct password can join the party.
- Clan Party: Only users belonging to the same Clan can join, without requiring a password.
⑥ Press the [Create Arena Party] button to complete the Arena party creation.
Arena Team Information
① You can view information about the members currently participating in the Arena party.
- The Party Leader can select party members to transfer leadership, remove members, or forcibly move them between teams.
② You can select [Leave Party] or [Move Team].
- These options are available to both the Party Leader and party members.
③ The Party Leader can press the [Start] button to begin the Arena match.
- When entering the Arena, 1 Arena entry count will be consumed for both the Party Leader and all party members.
④ The Party Leader can enable this option to automatically start the match as soon as all party slots are filled.
Arena Match Flow
① Upon entering the Arena, the character’s PK Mode will be fixed to Hostile.
② From the top left of the screen, you can use the [Leave] button to leave the Arena or check the waiting time and match duration.
- Each match begins after a 30-second waiting period and proceeds for 5 minutes.
③ No death penalties are applied while an Arena match is in progress.
④ If a character is defeated during combat, they will revive in the preparation area after 3 seconds and will no longer be able to participate in the ongoing match.
- Characters defeated in the Arena are not affected by revival or death delay effects from skills, Spirits, or items.
- If a character is revived through a skill or Spirit, they will revive immediately at the location of death and can rejoin the match.
⑤ After the match ends, players can view the results and the number of eliminations.
⑥ You can leave the Arena using the [Leave] button.
