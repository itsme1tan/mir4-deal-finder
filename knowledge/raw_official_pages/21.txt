Map
- Tap the top right area of the screen to view the map.
① You can check Merchant NPC, Resident NPC, landmark, gathering node, quarry, and monster information.
② You can choose a location on the map and travel there.
③ You can enable or disable Show Level. (Show Level will show monster levels and where they spawn)
④ You can view obtainable items.
Minimap
- You can check the minimap on the left of the main screen.
You can enable or disable the minimap display from Settings > Game > Convenience tab.
