WEMIX PAY Notes
What is WEMIX PAY?
- WEMIX PAY is a new payment method in WEMIX PLAY designed to lower onboarding platform fees for games while also offering in-game benefits for players. A portion of revenue from WEMIX PAY is used to support preserving the value of WEMIX, contributing to the sustainable growth of the WEMIX ecosystem.
- WEMIX PAY uses WEMIX Coin as its payment currency.
- Due to the nature of blockchain technology, purchases made with WEMIX PAY are non-refundable. Please keep this in mind when making purchases.
Menu
① Click the Shop icon in the top right corner to access the Shop.
- In the Shop, click the [WEMIX PAY] tab to switch to the WEMIX PAY payment method.
Purchase
Products in the in-game Shop and Event tabs can be purchased using WEMIX PAY.
[Shop]
1) Interface
① WEMIX PAY: Switches the payment method to WEMIX PAY.
② Cart: When using WEMIX PAY, the cart allows you to select and purchase multiple types of products in your desired quantities at once.
③ Storage: Products purchased using WEMIX PAY are delivered to your Storage.
2) Products
① To purchase multiple products at once, click the “Add to Cart” button.
② To purchase a single product, click on the desired item, review the purchase notice, and complete the transaction.
※ The 'Cart' feature will be added in a future update.
[Event Tab Products]
- Products such as Monthly Mission Scrolls and Attendance Event rewards can also be purchased using WEMIX PAY.
- Select the product you wish to purchase.
- On the payment screen, click WEMIX PAY to complete the purchase.
WEMIX PAY Notes
1. Products purchased with WEMIX PAY are non-refundable.
2. To use WEMIX PAY, you must link your WEMIX PLAY Wallet.
3. WEMIX PAY is only available on the official PC version.
4. Purchased items are delivered to your Storage.
5. Products purchased with currencies other than USD (Gold, Mileage, Copper, etc.) cannot be purchased using WEMIX PAY.
6. WEMIX PAY cannot be used while in the Domination Server, Expedition, or Valley of Life and Death.
7. Since WEMIX PAY reflects the real-time market price of WEMIX, the final payment amount may differ from the price displayed on the product.
