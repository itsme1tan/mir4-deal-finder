Mystery
Mysteries are unique chain quests where you unravel interesting cases hidden in the main story of Mir 4 in stages. You must talk to various people in the Land of Mir to obtain pieces of clues that will help you solve the quest.
Mystery
Mystery
- Tap the Mystery tab in the Menu.
- Views mysteries that are locked, the current stages of active mysteries, and completed mysteries.
- Each mystery stage gives a special reward, which can be registered in Codices.
- You receive additional rewards for reaching 100% progress.
