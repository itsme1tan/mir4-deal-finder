EXDRA
EXDRA (Expansion of DRACO) is a new seasonal token aimed at enhancing the value of existing tokens. Through PNIX DEX, users can trade their stored tokens with others at any time and use EXDRA as a material to craft powerful equipment.
[EXDRA Important Notice]
1. EXDRA is a seasonal token that can only be obtained during the season period.
2. A maximum issuance limit is set for each season, and once this limit is reached, EXDRA tokens can no longer be acquired.
3. Gas fees (WEMIX) are required when crafting EXDRA.
[EXDRA Primary Uses]
1. Crafting ‘Ancient Equipment’ in MIR4.
- Ancient Treasure
- Special Magical Soul Orb
2. Free trading through PNIX DEX.
- EXDRA tokens can be freely traded on PNIX DEX even after the season ends.

EXDRA Season4
The fourth season of the seasonal token EXDRA runs for 26 weeks, from after the July 7, 2026 update ~ before the January 5, 2027 update. The token name for EXDRA Season 4 is 'EXDRA4'.
[EXDRA4]
1. 7,000,000 EXDRA4 tokens are issued during the season period, and they can be obtained through MIR4’s in-game crafting.
[EXDRA4 Minting]
1. 'Jade Septaria' is used as an essential material in the EXDRA4 token Minting process.
2. The uses of Minted EXDRA4 tokens are as follows.
1) 'Ragnos's Earrings Box' and 'Gu Mado Magical Soul Orb Box' exchange material.
- It can be used as exchange material for boxes that allow you to obtain the new Ancient equipment 'Ragnos's Earrings' and the new Magical Soul Orb 'Gu Mado Magical Soul Orb'.
2) Free trading through PNIX DEX
[Jade Septaria]
1. Jade Septaria is an essential material for EXDRA4 token Minting.
- You can craft (Mint) EXDRA4 tokens at [Menu > XDRACO > Season Token > Crafting Station].
- EXDRA4 Recipe: Jade Septaria x5 / Copper x10,000
2. Jade Septaria can be obtained through the methods below.
- Mission Scroll and Monthly Check-in Rewards
- Limited Product Purchases
- Transference Equipment Enhancement Failure (Destruction) Rewards (+4 or Higher)
- All-Server Power Score Ranking Rewards
- Disenchant Rewards
- Field Boss Defeat Rewards
- Conquest Treasure Rewards
※ Please refer to [Game Encyclopedia - Conquest Treasure] for more information.
[EXDRA4 Exchange]
1. List of items that can be exchanged using EXDRA4 tokens:
- Ragnos's Earrings Box
- Gu Mado Magical Soul Orb Box
2. Ragnos's Earrings Box
- Ragnos's Earrings Box Exchange Recipe: EXDRA4 Token x3
- When using the 'Ragnos's Earrings Box', you can randomly obtain one of various items, including Ragnos's Earrings, Ancient Treasure Enhancement Stone, and Ancient Treasure Enchant Scroll.
※ Refer to [Game Encyclopedia - Ancient Treasure] for details.
3. Gu Mado Magical Soul Orb Box
- Gu Mado Magical Soul Orb Box Exchange: EXDRA4 Token x2
- When using the 'Gu Mado Magical Soul Orb Box', you can randomly obtain one of various items, including Gu Mado Magical Soul Orb, Goblin, Magical Soul Orb Enchant Scroll, and Special Magical Soul Orb Enchant Scroll.
※ Refer to [Game Encyclopedia - Magical Soul Orb] for details.
EXDRA Crafting
[EXDRA Menu]
1. The EXDRA menu is accessible from Level 40.
2. Menu Access Method
1) Game Menu (+) > XDRACO > Season Token
① Game Menu (+) > Click ‘XDRACO’
② Click the ‘Season Token’ button.
2) If a WEMIX PLAY account is not linked, a pop-up will guide you to link your account.
※ Each WEMIX PLAY account can be linked to only one game account, and once linked, it cannot be unlinked.
3) After linking your WEMIX PLAY account, the EXDRA balance owned in your WEMIX PLAY App will be displayed.
[EXDRA Crafting Precautions]
- A maximum of 1,000 EXDRA tokens can be crafted per account per day.
- The daily reset time is 00:00 (UTC+8).
- Crafting will be unavailable once the season’s maximum issuance limit is reached.
[EXDRA Crafting]
1) Go to [Game Menu (+) > XDRACO > Season Token > Crafting Station] in-game.
2) You can check the number of tokens you currently have, the materials required to craft tokens, the token issuance amount, the end of the season, and the remaining daily crafting amount.
3) Enter the crafting amount, check the required materials, and then press the [Craft] button to craft.
※ If your WEMIX PLAY App account is not linked, crafting will proceed after linking your account.
4) Results from crafting will be delivered via in-game mail.
EXDRA Exchange
[EXDRA Exchange]
1) Go to [Game Menu (+) > XDRACO > Season Token > Exchange Station] in-game.
2) You can check the list of exchangeable items, the number of tokens required to craft the item, and the number of tokens you have.
3) Enter the exchange amount, check the required materials, and click the [Exchange] button to proceed with the exchange.
※ If your WEMIX PLAY App account is not linked, the exchange will proceed after linking the account.
4) Results of the exchange and the items obtained will be delivered via in-game mail.

[EXDRA1 Minting]
1. When minting EXDRA1 Tokens, the materials: ‘Red Nebula Powder’ and ‘Red Septaria.’ are required.
2. The uses for minted EXDRA1 tokens are as follows:
1) Crafting materials for ‘Ancient Equipment Box’ and ‘Special Magical Soul Orb Box.’
- You can obtain ‘Ancient Treasure’ and ‘Special Magical Soul Orb’ by using ‘Ancient Equipment Box’ and ‘Special Magical Soul Orb Box.’
2) Freely Trade on PNIX DEX.
3. The minting process for EXDRA1 is as follows:
1) Use Red Nebula Powder to craft a ‘Flame Bead.’
2) Use ‘Flame Bead’ to obtain ‘Red Septaria.’
3) Use ‘Red Septaria’ to craft EXDRA1 tokens.
[Materials for EXDRA1 Token Minting]
The materials required for EXDRA1 token minting are as follows:
1. Red Nebula Powder
1) Required for crafting Flame Bead, which yields Red Septaria.
2) Red Nebula Powder can be acquired through:
- Mission Scroll rewards.
- Specific limited item purchases.
- Enhancement Great Failure (destruction) of Transference Equipment (Enhancement Stage 3 or higher).
2. Flame Bead
1) You can randomly obtain Red Septaria by using Flame Bead.
2) Flame Bead crafting requirements:
- 5x Red Nebula Powder, 1x Rare Pomegranate Flower, and 10,000 Energy.
- Crafting NPC: Sanctuary of Hydra NPC <Flame Bead Craftsman> Poipoi
<Flame Bead Craftsman> Poipoi
3. Red Septaria
1) Red Septaria is required for crafting EXDRA1 tokens.
2) Red Septaria can be acquired through:
- All Servers Power Score Ranking rewards.
- Flame Bead.
[Ancient Equipment Box and Special Magical Soul Orb Box]
1) Items that can be exchanged with EXDRA1 tokens include 'Ancient Equipment Box' and 'Special Magical Soul Orb Box.'
2) Ancient Equipment Box
- Ancient Equipment Box Crafting Recipe: 3x EXDRA1 tokens.
- By using ‘Ancient Equipment Box,’ you have a chance to randomly obtain one of several items, including ‘Ancient Treasure Ragnos'sRing’, ‘Ancient Treasure EnhancementStone,’ or ‘Ancient Treasure Enchant Scroll’.
※ Refer to [Game Encyclopedia - Ancient Treasure] for details.
3) Special Magical Soul Orb Box
- Special Magical Soul Orb Box Crafting Recipe: 2x EXDRA1 tokens.
- By using ‘Special Magical Soul Orb Box,’ you have a chance to randomly obtain one of several items, including ‘Sealed Magical Soul Orb,’ ‘Goblin,’ ‘Magical Soul Orb Enchant Scroll,’ or ‘Special Magical Soul Orb Enchant Scroll.’
※ Refer to [Game Encyclopedia - Magical Soul Orb] for details.
