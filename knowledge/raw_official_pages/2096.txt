Redmoon Purgatory
Redmoon Purgatory is a labyrinth-type region where players can obtain large amounts of equipment and materials, including the Burnt Skill Tome and Ancient/Skill Arcane Tomes, which are used in the Scripture Hall.

Entering Redmoon Purgatory
- Redmoon Purgatory is accessible to characters who have reached Level 120 or higher.
- You can enter Redmoon Purgatory from the [Spiritual Center] region.
- Each floor of Redmoon Purgatory has its own level requirement.

Redmoon Purgatory Info
1. Quest Boss Monsters
- Each floor of Redmoon Purgatory contains a Quest Boss Monster.
- Quest Boss Monsters appear four times daily (06:00, 12:00, 18:00, and 24:00 server time).
- Each Quest Boss remains active for 5 minutes after spawning. If not defeated within that time, it will disappear automatically.
- Defeating a Quest Boss will spawn a treasure chest containing various items.
2. Summon Boss Monsters
- Floors 1 through 6 of Redmoon Purgatory feature special zones called ‘Hidden Sector’ and ‘Forbidden Sector,’ where boss monsters can be summoned using dedicated summoning items.
- Summoning badges for ‘Hidden Sector’ and ‘Forbidden Sector’ zones can be purchased from NPC merchants or obtained as rewards from Repeatable Achievements.
- Summoned bosses in these zones must be defeated within a limited time, or they will disappear.
- Monthly Repeatable Missions can be completed in the Hidden/Forbidden Sectors. These missions reset every month on the 1st at 00:00 (UTC+8).
3. Special Boss Monster
- On Redmoon Purgatory 7F, you can encounter the special boss <Hellish Lord> Helbar.
- Helbar spawns every Wednesday at 23:00 (UTC+8).
- Defeating Helbar spawns a treasure chest with various items, including a low chance to obtain an Ancient Box, which can be opened using the Rare Ancient Key.
4. Boss Monster Rewards
- Defeating normal, quest, or special boss monsters offers a chance to obtain a Burnt Skill Tome.
- Quest and special boss monsters also have a low chance of dropping Ancient/Skill Arcane Tomes.
