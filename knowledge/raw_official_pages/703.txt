World Boss
World Boss is a boss monster that spawns in random servers with a certain chance at a designated schedule.
It is a weekly content that all Natives and Expedition members can participate in through the expedition.
Nerkan/Turkan
Drakazan
Nerkan/Turkan
1. Basic Information
- Black Flame Arch Demon Nerkan will spawn in a random server as Sealed on Tuesday, and Violet Demon God Turkan will spawn with the same condition on Thursday.
- Both Nerkan and Turkan will become active for battle from Sealed status at 23:00.
※ The schedule will be determined based on Regional Server Time.
※ There will be servers where the boss does not appear.
■ Black Flame Arch Demon Nerkan
■ Violet Demon God Turkan
2. Spawn Area
- [Black Flame Arch Demon Nerkan] : The boss will spawn in 'Black Flame Abaddon', which can be entered from the portal located on Redmoon Valley 4F
- [Violet Demon God Turkan] : The boss will spawn in 'Violet Abaddon, which can be entered from the portal located on Phantasia Valley 4F.
※ Criteria: All players who are located in a server where the World Boss has spawned. (Including players who moved by expedition)
3. Areal Trait
- Maximum of 450 players can be entered in this area and players over this number will be enlisted to the waiting queue.
- PK is enabled in this area, there is no reduction of alignment points due to the PK. (Loss of EXP will still be affected upon death)
- If a player dies not by PK, death penalties such as loss of EXP and items and extension of revival cooldown will be applied.
■ Black Flame Abaddon (Black Flame Arch Demon Nerkan)
■ Violet Abaddon (Violet Demon God Turkan)
4. Notification
- An alert message shown below will appear every Tuesday and Thursday when the World Boss spawns at 19:00 of regional server times.
If the boss spawns in your server
If the boss spawns in a different server
- After checking the spawned server, Expedition players can access the content through the following: [Transfer] menu > Expedition > Visiting Server.
※ Expedition allows players only to move within the same region.
5. Seal Status
- Red Background  : Unsealed status of the World Boss
- Orange Background : Sealed status of the World Boss
Drakazan
1. Default Information
- Drakazon will be spawned as 'Dormant' at 02:00 every Monday.
- Dormant will be lifted on a random day at 04:10 am except for the weekend, and the status will be switched to 'Standby'.
- The Standby status will be changed to 'Appear' on the appearance day at 9:10 pm, and it can be encountered upon entering the Domination Server.
※ The Spawn time will be determined based on Regional Server Times.
■ Blackhorn Winged Drakin Drakazan
2. Spawn Area
- [Blackhorn Winged Drakin Drakazan]: It will be spawned at Blackdragon Abaddon where you can enter through the portal located at Black Dragon Valley 4F in Domination Server.
※ Criteria: All users entering into Domination Server on the appearance day
3. Areal Trait
- Maximum of 450 players can be entered in this area and players over this number will be enlisted into the waiting queue.
- Upon death, Death Penalty including EXP/Item loss, and extended cooldown on revival will be applied. (There is no deduction of alignment points upon death by PK)
■ Black Dragon Abaddon (Blackhorn Winged Drakin Drakazan)
4. Detailed Information
- Drakazan’s ‘current status’ information can be checked in the window before entering the domination server.
- The boss monster will switch its status among Dormant, Standby, and Appear at a designated schedule and become the Vanquished status.
※ Each Status and schedule will be determined by Regional Server Times.
① You can check Drakazan's 'Dormant' status on every Monday, at 2:00 am.
② You can check Drakazan's 'Standby' status on random days except for weekends, at 4:10 am.
③ You can check Drakazan's 'Appear' status on the day it is stated as 'Standby' at 9:10 pm.
④ You can check Drakazan’s ‘Vanquished’ status
5. Precautions
- Drakazan is normally armed with a high value of ATK. Depending on the amount of remaining HP, a special pattern will be initiated or various monsters will be summoned
- Defeating Drakazan is a competition against every player who can enter the Domination Server, cooperation will be necessary for players on the same side.
- Be aware that Drakazan will go dormant and disappear if it is not defeated until the next Monday.
- A debuff 'Hemotoxin Host' will be enchanted to players in constant, and you will be under Instant Death when the debuff accumulated has reached its thresholds.
- Hemotoxin Host debuff can be lifted through a special method in Black Dragon Abaddon.
