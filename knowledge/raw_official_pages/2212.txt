Altar of Sublimation
The Altar of Sublimation is a new system that allows you to use Legendary-grade Magic Stones / Spectrumite / Spirit Treasures as materials to attempt to obtain Mythic-grade Magic Stones / Spectrumite / Spirit Treasures.
Basic Information of the Altar of Sublimation

Basic Information of the Altar of Sublimation
1. You can access the Altar of Sublimation through the Spiritual Center NPC ‘<Altar of Sublimation> Enlightened Great Matron’.
2. To perform Sublimation, you must use three Legendary Magic Stones / Spectrumite / Spirit Treasures of the same tier, along with Darksteel and Dragonsteel.
3. The higher the Special Enhancement level of the material items, the higher the success rate of Sublimation.
4. If you register two or more of the same type of item, the chance of obtaining that specific type upon success will increase.
- Example: Registering two or more Legendary Magic Stones of Vigor will increase the chance of obtaining a Mythic Magic Stone of Vigor when successful
5. Mythic-grade items obtained through Sublimation are non-tradable.
How to Perform Altar of Sublimation
① Select the material items for Altar of Sublimation.
- Three Legendary Magic Stones / Spectrumite / Spirit Treasures of the same tier are required as materials.
② Use the button at the bottom left to configure whether tradable items can be used.
- This prevents mistakes of accidentally using tradable items as materials.
③ You can check the items registered as materials in the center of the screen.
- If Sublimation fails, all materials except for the first registered item (center) will disappear.
④ Check the success rate and possible obtainable items.
- The higher the Special Enhancement level of the registered items, the greater the chance of success.
- The success rate is calculated as the sum of the success rates of the registered items.

Crafting Mythic Magic Stones / Spectrumite / Spirit Treasure Chests
- You can craft guaranteed chests that yield Mythic Magic Stones / Spectrumite / Spirit Treasures through the NPC <Dragonsteel Merchant> Bunny Guru Hoppy in the Arcadia Spirit Village.
