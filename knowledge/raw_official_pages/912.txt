Mystique
An upgrading system that additionally increases character abilities can be activated upon reaching level 90.
Mystique
Mystique
- A Content that is available for characters over level 90 only.
- Tap the Mystique - Conquest in the Menu.
- There are 5 Mystiques in total, and each of them can be upgraded up to Stage 10.
① View the [Power Score] promoted by Mystique at the top left.
② View the [Mystique Stage] and [Applied Stat] at the top right.
- Mystique will start from Stage 1 after unlocking the feature, and Mystique's blessing will be applied after promoting to Stage 2.
- When upgraded, one of the four Fixed Stats will be randomly increased.
※ All four Fixed stats can be enhanced up to 5 stages maximum and Promotion will be available when succeeding in upgrading all four Fixed Stats in each stage.
③ The blessing level of the Mystique can be viewed from the bottom center of the screen.
- Stats and Blessing level will be further upgraded per 2 promotion stages. (stage 2,4,6,8,10)
