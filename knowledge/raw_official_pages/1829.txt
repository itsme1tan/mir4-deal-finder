WEMIX PLAY Webshop
The WEMIX PLAY Webshop is a webshop within the blockchain game platform, offering exclusive, special items not available in the NIGHT CROWS in-game shop.
Basic Information
Usage
Precautions
Basic Information
- The WEMIX PLAY Webshop is divided into two sections: Item shop (WEMIX PAY) and PLAY Token shop (PLAY Token), each with its exclusive products.
[WEMIX PLAY MIR4 Webshop]
1. WEMIX PAY
- WEMIX PAY is a new payment method for WEMIX PLAY that reduces commission rates for onboarding game platforms, offers users exclusive in-game benefits, and support sustainable growth of the WEMIX ecosystem by allocating a portion of its revenue to preserve WEMIX value.
- Payments made via WEMIX PAY use WEMIX coin, and users can earn additional  WEMIX Pay Mileage, which is required for PLAY Token minting when purchasing products.
- Please note that due to the nature of blockchain technology, products purchased via WEMIX PLAY cannot be refunded.
2. PLAY Token
- PLAY Token is a new utility token within the WEMIX ecosystem that serves as a payment method for various games on WEMIX PLAY.
- WEMIX Pay Mileage, which can be earned through making payments on WEMIX PAY, can be exchanged for PLAY Tokens.
- The Webshop drives demand by offering certain game items exclusively purchasable with PLAY Tokens while preventing inflation through the “use and burn” principle.
- Please note that due to the nature of blockchain technology, products purchased via Play Token cannot be refunded.
Usage
- You may access the products on WEMIX PLAY Webshop via the following three methods.
1. Connect to the WEMIX PLAY Official Homepage or the App.
- Check the Webshop list on the left tab and click the MIR4 Webshop button
- Select the shop you wish to use (Item Shop / PLAY Token Shop) and purchase the desired item products.
2. Connect to the official MIR4 forum
- Click the WEMIX PLAY Webshop button located on the top right side.
3. Connect via in-game
- Click the cart icon located at the top-right corner, select the Web Shop button from the top menu, choose the products you wish to purchase by selecting a payment method, click the purchase button, and proceed to the WEMIX PLAY Webshop.
Precautions
- Items purchased from the Webshop will be sent to the in-game mail of the Main Character associated with the account linked to WEMIX PLAY.
- Please note that due to the nature of blockchain technology, products purchased from the WEMIX PLAY Webshop cannot be refunded.
- A gas fee(WEMIX) will be consumed when exchanging WEMIX PAY Mileage for Play Tokens.
- For items with daily, weekly, or monthly purchase limits, if the page is closed before the purchase is completed, the purchase count will be applied. If this issue occurs, please wait for a short period before attempting to complete the purchase again.
