Clan Match
Clan Match is a tournament style combat where a total of 32 Clans battle each other in the Domination Server to determine the strongest Clan.

Clan Match Information
1. Basic Information
- Clan Match takes place in the Domination server. Characters must be level 50 and above to participate.
- During the Clan Match Application Period, 32 Clans will be selected and then the finalized bracket will be revealed.
- Clan Match will commence under a schedule with the selected Clans throughout 3 weeks.
- Rewards will be distributed for the winner and loser of each Clan Match Round. Rewards for individual Dragonians and MVP Rewards will also be distributed.
- Among the obtainable rewards, Dragonians can use the Guardian's Badge to exchange or craft items.
- Clan Match will commence every 8 weeks.

Apply for a Clan Match
1. Procedures to Apply① Apply for a Clan Match by going to the following: [Menu >War > Clan Match > Apply > ‘Apply’ button]
② Remaining time left to apply for Clan Match and prerequisites to apply is being displayed.
- The following prerequisites must be met before being able to apply for a Clan Match.
1) Clan Technique > Clan development’s Commanding Officer level 5 and above.
2) Clan Leader or a Clan member with war permission.
③ Clan Match Bracket can be checked.
④ Clan Match Rewards can be checked.
- A ‘Regular Reward’ is obtained for each Round your Clan has won or lost. An individual Reward is granted depending on the amount of points the Dragonian has obtained.
⑤ Dragonians can check the final winner of the Clan Match
2. Bracket- Clan Match’s Bracket consists of 32 Clans.
- If more than 32 Clans have applied for the Clan Match, the top 32 Clans with the highest Power Score among the Clans that have applied, will be selected.
- Rounds will decrease if less than 32 Clans have applied.

Clan Match Procedure
1. Match Preparation
- Clan Match will commence on the 1st ~ 3rd Week on Saturday, 9:00 ~ 9:45 / 10:00 ~ 10:45. For each Round, a 5 minute wait time will take place before the Match. (Each Region’s standard time.)
- Before entering the Clan Match, the Clan Leader or the Clan member with War Permission can set and save the ‘Commanding Officer’ Skill through the ‘Enter Match’ window.
2. Match Begin
- Obtain points in Clan Match by defeating opposing Clan members / obtaining territories / defeating monsters. Clan Match consists of 5 types of monsters and 6 obtainable territories.
- When the Match is over, the Clan with the most points wins the Clan Match.
- The ‘Combat Status Board’ as displayed in the image below, can be checked once entering the Clan Match.
① Each side’s Clan Name, points, and remaining time can be checked.
② When the Clan Match begins, Tactical Points will continue to replenish. Use the points and the Commanding Officer Skills to grant all Clan members a buff or grant all opposing Clan members a debuff.
- Commanding Officer Skill can only be used by Clan Leaders or Clan Members with War Permission.
③ The Clan Leader and the Clan member with War Permission can make a variety orders with Tactical Markers to Clan members through the Combat Status Board
- Types of Markers: Attack, Rally, Defend, Retreat, Stand by
④ Click on the Ranking button to display a window showing current rankings of all individual Clan members’ points
⑤ Chat log with Clan members is displayed.

5. Rules
- Clan Match will commence as a 50:50 Dragonians on the battlefield, totaling the capacity of participants to be 100 Dragonians.
- All Clan members that meet the prerequisites to participate in the Clan Match can freely enter the domination server during the Clan Match.
- If the Clan Match is entered while in a Party, the Dragonian will automatically leave the Party. (Excluding Clan Party)
- All buffs will be removed when entering the Clan Match, except for all Clan Skill and Altar of Black Dragon. (ex- Title, Position, and Server Buffs.)
- Disband Clan, Leave Clan, Kick will not be available when in the Clan Match map and Clan alliances will not be applied when in a Clan Match. All targets except for Clan members will be considered an enemy.
- PVP Log, Bounty Hunt, Wanted List, Clan Target does not apply within the Clan Match’s map
- Duels are unavailable in the Clan Match area.
- ‘Life’ will not be used and Raid/Expedition Tokens can not be obtained when in the Clan Match map.
- If the Point difference between the 2 Clans surmounts to 10,000 points during a Clan Match, the Clan with the higher points will automatically become the winner.
Clan Match Composition
1. Occupying Territories
- There are a total of 6 Occupied Territories and 6 monoliths.
- Occupied Territories are located in the higher areas of the Temple/Shrine. Dragonians must utilize Air Walk and the neutral monster (Giant Rock Fiend).
- Methods to occupy each territory are different.

- The Occupy Gauge in the Monolith of Rallying will be filled for the Clan that has the most surviving Clan members in the vicinity.
- If the opposing Clan is already Occupying the Monolith of Rallying, the filled Gauge must first be decreased before filling the gauge for your allied Clan.
- It is advantageous to have more Clan members when attacking and occupying the Monolith of Restoration because the damage taken by the Monolith is limited.
- The last Clan member that hit the Monolith of Restoration to its destruction will occupy the territory. The monolith will respawn with a certain amount of HP.
- The respawned Monolith of Restoration will continue to regenerate HP. The amount of HP restored will depend on the amount of surviving allied Clan members that are in the vicinity of the Monolith.
2. Monster
- In the Clan Match, there are 3 types of Watchkeepers, 1 type of Guardian,  1 type of Neutral Monster, which totals to 5 types of monsters.
- The 3 Watchkeeper Monsters are: Iron Wall / Destruction / Gale Watchkeeper, and they have a Beast and a Demon Beast variant.
- All Watchkeepers spawn 3 times during the Clan Match. If the Monster is not defeated within 5 minutes of its spawn, it will disappear.
- The Clan that defeated the Watchkeeper will gain a buff effect for 5 minutes.

- Clan Match’s Guardian is the ‘Guardian of the Ruins’.
- When the Guardian of the Ruins appears, a special area around the occupied Temple/Shrine appears as well.
- The Guardian of the Ruins can not be defeated by attacking it.
- Defeat the Guardian of the Ruins by getting the attention of the Guardian and have it follow you to the special area
- Clan Match’s Neutral Monster is the ‘Giant Rock Fiend’.
- There are 4 Giant Rock Fiend’s spawn location. The Giant Rock fiend can be jumped on to access the occupied Territory.
- The Giant Rock Fiend can be defeated by attacking it. It will respawn after 5 minutes.
