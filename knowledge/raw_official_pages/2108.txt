Path of Fiery Battle
Path of Fiery Battle is a training ground created for those who seek to reach the legendary pinnacle of martial arts. Only the most seasoned Dragonians who have overcome countless challenges across the continent of Mir may attempt this ultimate trial.
[Path of Fiery Battle Overview]
- Path of Fiery Battle is a Raid content available to characters of Level 120 or higher.
- It consists of 6 Acts, and each Act contains 10 Scenes.
- Players can take on the challenge alone or in a party of up to 5 members.
[How to Enter Path of Fiery Battle]
- You can access the content via [Menu > Raid > Path of Fiery Battle].
① View the list of Scenes within each Act along with their special rewards.
- Special rewards can be obtained only once by clearing specific Scenes for the first time and will be delivered via in-game mail.
② Check detailed information about the selected Scene and add it to your favorites.
③ View the reward list available for the selected Scene and check your current and required number of Path of Fiery Battle Tickets.
④ Use the [Search Raids] button to join an existing party, or [Create a Raid] to form your own.
⑤ View information about current party members or invite others by clicking the (+) icon.
⑥ If the ‘Auto-Start When Party Is Full’ option is enabled, the raid will begin automatically once 5 members have joined.
⑦ Use [Disband] to dissolve your party or press [Start] to manually begin the battle.
[Path of Fiery Battle Progression]
- Each raid is composed of a sequence of training stages. You must complete all stages within the time limit.
① Once you enter, the raid will begin automatically after a short waiting period. Complete all missions within the given time to succeed.
② Use the buttons on the right to check your loot or exit the raid immediately.
- Tickets consumed upon entry will not be refunded if you log out or leave before completing the challenge.
③ If your character dies during battle, you will respawn in a safe zone after the resurrection cooldown.
- No EXP is lost upon death in Path of Fiery Battle.
- If all party members are defeated, the stage progress will be preserved, but the enemy’s state will reset.
④ Check your completion rewards.
- First-time clear rewards will be delivered by mail and the next Scene will become available.
⑤ Use the buttons at the bottom of the screen to check the rewards earned by your party or leave the raid.
[Trainee's Crescent Jade Exchange]
- Clearing Path of Fiery Battle will earn you the ‘Trainee’s Crescent Jade’ item. These can be exchanged for various rewards through the NPC <Path of Fiery Battle CDR> Park Hee, located in Bicheon Castle and Sabuk Castle.
