Unified Market
Dragonians who are eager to become the ruler of mir continent need not only Power Score but also advanced political senses and abundant resources are required.
The foundation of all these factors lies with the Unified Market, which makes you trade with other Dragonians in the same region as a core of the economy.

Basic Information
Unified Market is a market system enabling the trade with Dragoninas of other servers within your region.
- Requirement: Unlocked upon completing the quest 'Escape Gingko Valley'
- Level Requirement for Selling : 40
[Caution]
1. It cannot be used while using Expedition or Domination Server.
2. The minimum tax charge is 1 Gold.
3. Amount of Unified Market taxes can be deducted depending on the Tower of Conquest stage.
4. You can either register or purchase products from servers within your region.
(E.g: ASIA1 (All servers belonging to the ASIA1 region, from ASIA011 to ASIA043)
Interface
* Tap the Unified Market in the in-game menu to move to the Unified Market windows.
① Unified Market feature will be implemented in each tab (Search/Register for Sales/Settlement/Settlement History/Purchase History).
② You can search or filter items on sale with the following option.
③ Choose the category on the left side to check the list of items for sale.
④ You can view the sales list and minimum price in the Register for Sales.
- Up to 60 items can be registered or excluded to Favoirte with the (★) icon located at the left.
- Click the item icon at the left to view its details.
(For equipment items, you can compare the stats of the listed one to that of equipped.
Purchase
① Click the registered item in Unified Market to make a purchase or to view the average price, purchase quantity, and price.
② You can view the remaining time for registered items.
Selling
① You can check tradable items or register items for sale in Register for Sale tab.
- Copper will cost as a registration fee.
② Your registration will be made after a certain period of waiting time and can be canceled with the Cancel button.
- If your registration has expired or canceled on your own, items can be retrieved from Register for Sale tab, however, the registration fee will not be reimbursed.
Settlement
① Click the Settlement button on the Settlement tab to claim the revenue except for tax charges with the Market Tax rate.
② Click the (i) icon located at the top right to view the Market Tax rate.
