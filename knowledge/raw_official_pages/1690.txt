Cosmic Harmony
Cosmic Harmony is growth content that can further strengthen characters who have reached high levels of Solitude Training.
Cosmic Harmony
Full Renewal
Cosmic Harmony
- Cosmic Harmony is unlocked when Limbo Realm Stage 1 (168 Tier or higher) is achieved in Solitude Training.
- Cosmic Harmony can be found in ‘Menu > Conquests’.
① Consists of ‘Cosmic Harmony’, which allows you to train your character, and ‘Full Renewal’, which allows you to change the Celestial Cycle effect obtained from Cosmic Harmony.
② The Power Score that increases through Cosmic Harmony is displayed, and you can check detailed stats by clicking the magnifying glass button on the right.
③ Cosmic Harmony consists of a total of 4 Trainings: ‘Heaven and Earth (I) / Heavenly Worship (II) / One Earth (III) / United Protection (IV)’.
- There are 8 types of stats that can be upgraded sequentially in each Training List.

※ Whenever a stat reaches the maximum upgradeable level, the next stat is unlocked.
④ By clicking the Stats icon, you can check the materials consumed during Training, the stats increase value, and the probability of success and Tangled Inner Force.
- When you proceed with Training, an outcome of ‘Great Success/Success/Failure/Tangled Inner Force’ will occur. If Tangled Inner Force occurs, your Training level will decrease and you will be unable to train for a certain period of time.
- Wayfarer Travel and NFT Character Sealing is unavailable when Tangled Inner Force or Celestial Cycle promotion is in progress.
- The duration of Tangled Inner Force's effect can be shortened through Clan Support, Gold, and Speed-ups.
⑤ When all 8 types of stats reach the maximum upgradeable level, you can Promote the Celestial Cycle Stage.
⑥ You can view the materials required to Promote.
- If you proceed with a promotion, a promotion waiting period will be applied and you will not be able to use Wayfarer Travel.
- The promotion waiting time can be shortened by using Gold, Speed-ups, or Clan support.
- Once the promotion is completed, one of the ‘Celestial Cycle Effect List’ will be applied randomly.
- You can obtain one Celestial Cycle Effect each time you Promote each stage.
- The same properties do not appear repeatedly in the Celestial Cycle Effect.
⑦ You can check the currently applied Celestial Cycle Effect at the bottom left of the screen, and check detailed stats through the magnifying glass button.

Full Renewal
- With Full Renewal, the Celestial Cycle effect obtained from Cosmic Harmony can be changed to other stats.
① You can move to the Full Renewal window.
② You can select the Training List that requires Full Renewal.
③ When performing Full Renewal for the selected Training List, you can check the necessary materials and the applied Celestial Cycle Effect.
- Full Renewal is available from Stage 1 of the Celestial Cycle.
- If you proceed with Full Renewal, the type and grade of all Stats will be changed at once.
- Full Renewal uses Celestial Soul Pill/Dragonsteel/Energy, and Celestial Soul Pill can be obtained by crafting through an NPC.
- When crafting a Celestial Soul Pill through the NPC <Palace Craftsman> Hyo Janggyeong, there is a 10% chance to obtain a tradeable Celestial Soul Pill .

④ You will be moved to a window where you can change the Celestial Cycle effect.
⑤ In Full Renewal, all stats are changed randomly, and the changed stats can be applied through the [Apply] button.
- A total of 21 types of stats appear in each list, and depending on the probability, you can obtain properties up to SS grade.
⑥ You can compare ‘Current Stats’ and ‘Change Stats’ every time you attempt Full Renewal.
⑦ By setting the quantity of the target grade (SS/S), Full Renewal can be automatically attempted repeatedly until the target result is achieved.
- Repeat attempts can be set up to 999 times at a time.
