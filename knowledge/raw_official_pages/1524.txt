Transference Equipment
Equipment that embeds the force of masters from another dimension, granting you enormous strength. It is categorized as a Secondary Weapon like Magic Stone and Mystical Piece, and inner stats can be upgraded through Enhancement and Enchant.

Transference Equipment
1.  Basic Information
- Item Description: Gifts embedding inner forces of people in World 4 Layer 8 connected through Mirage Ship can be used for your Character Growth.
- Level Requirement: Character Level 100 or higher
- Obtaining Method: Transference Equipment can be purchased from the In-game Shop or NPC <Darksteel Exchanger> located at Bicheon Castle/Snake Pit/Spiritual Center
2. Interface
- You can access the Transference Equipment tab through [Character > Secondary Weapon] on the menu.
① You can view power score and stats applied by equipping the Transference Equipment.
② There are a total of 10 slots for Transcendence Equipment, with 5 equipment slots available per page.
Locked slots will be gradually unlocked through future updates.
③ You can check the equipped status of Transcendence Equipment.
④ You can view the Transference Equipment that you own.
⑤ Click the Transference Equipment to view its details.
- Transference Equipment has hidden stats and passive skills which are activated upon reaching a corresponding Enhancement Stage.
※ Precautions
Transference Equipment can be used in common for every class of MIR4.
It will be transferred upon taking the Wayfarer Travel or NFT Sealing.
Consume the exclusive material 'Transference Equipment Enhancement Stone' for Enhancement.
Consume the exclusive item 'Transference Enchant Scroll' for Enchant
Combining / Special Enhancement / Dragon Forge is unavailable for the Transference Equipment.
Enhancement
① Choose your targeted Transference Equipment at the right of the game screen.
- It consumes Legendary Transference Equipment Enhancement Stone, Darksteel, and Copper.
② You can view the chance to succeed and increase in stats per each Enhancement Stage.
- Failure Chance will be applied from Enhancement Stage 2, and it will be destroyed upon resulting in Failure.
- Hidden Stats will be activated upon reaching Enhancement Stage 3/6/9/12, and Passive skill will be activated upon reaching Enhancement Stage 8.
- After getting activated, hidden stats will be upgraded depending on the corresponding Enhancement Stage.
- Up to Stage 12 can be enhanced as Max.
- You can view a related achievement that can obtain rewards with the following failure counts in a specific Enhancement Stage through Menu > Mission > Achievement > Transference Equipment.
③ Click the Enhance button or use Auto-Enhance to upgrade your Transference Equipment(s) to your goals.

Enchant
① Choose the targeted Transference Equipment at the right of the game screen.
② You can view the status and requirements for Enchant.
③ Access the Enchant windows by clicking the Enchant button.
④ Upon enchanting, you can compare the changes in stats before and after at the right of the game screen.
⑤ Additional stats will be randomized, and they won’t be applied until clicking the Apply button.
⑥ You can view required currencies and Max grade possibility for Enchant.
⑦ You can set the goals for a specific grade and repeat your Enchant automatically until reaching the goal.
- Up to 999 Auto-Enchant can be set.
