Craft
In Craft, you can use materials obtained by hunting or summoning to craft new equipment, combine equipment to obtain higher grade equipment, or exchange items.

Crafting
- Tap Craft - Crafting tab in the Menu to open the Exchange window.
① You can craft equipment, medicine, equipment materials, promotion materials, or Codex armor.
② Tap the Enhance button in Crafting Equipment to open Forge > Enhance window.
③ Selecting a crafting material shows how it can be obtained.
④ Crafting requires Copper, and Darksteel or Energy may also be used depending on the recipe.
⑤ When crafting a Rare or higher equipment, tier 4 & enhancement level 8+ equipment of the previous grade can replace key materials.
Combine
- Tap Craft > Combine tab in the Menu.
- Combine Types

Combine Unidentified
Unidentified Magic Stone / Unidentified Spirit Treasure / Unidentified Codex Gear / Unidentified Mystical Piece
※ Epic Combine is unavailable for Unidentified Codex Gear / Mystical Piece. (Impossible to acquire Legendary grade)
※ Items with ‘Tradable’ and ‘XDRACO’ properties can’t be obtained through these combines.
① You can combine Dragon Materials, Spirit Stones, Skill Tomes, Spirit Treasures, Magic Stones, and Unidentified items.
② Combining requires 4 items of the same type and grade.
③ Choose sets of 4 items of the same type/grade that you want to combine, then tap the Combine button to combine them.
④ Combining yields a same grade item or a higher-grade item at a certain chance.
Combine - Mystic Incense Burner
- When combining Rare/Epic items fails, you gain 1 Mystic Incense Burner point.
When combining 4 Rare Spirit Stones fails, you gain 1 Spirit Stone Epic Incense Burner point. When combining 8 Epic Spirit Stones fails, you gain 2 Spirit Stone Legendary Incense Burner points.
- When you obtain 25 Incense Burner Points, you will receive the guaranteed reward for the grade you attempted.
- For guaranteed rewards, the Combine I and Combine Unidentified categories guarantee the grade you attempted, while the Combine II category grants a random reward from among the grades you attempted.
Unseal
- Tap Craft - Unseal tab in the Menu to open the Unseal window.
- You can unseal sealed boxes in the Unseal window.
- Unsealing time can be reduced by receiving Clan Support or using Speed-ups.
Exchange
- Tap Craft > Exchange tab in the Menu.
- Equipment can be exchanged for items of the same grade and tier. Skill Tomes and Spirit Stones are exchanged for random items of the same grade.
- Exchanging an equipment resets its enhancement level and enchantment.
Special Exchange
Special Exchange is a feature that can be used to exchange other classes' equipment with the current class of the character.
1.  Special Exchange
- Tap Craft > Exchange > Special Exchange tab from the menu to move to the Special Exchange screen.
ⓛ You can select the equipment to exchange into from the left side of the screen.
- Special Exchange can be used with the Epic tier equipment or above.
- Equipment that is locked/Time Limited, or that did not complete Enchant/Special Enchant can not be registered to be exchanged.
- Special Exchange can be used to change equipment (Weapon, Secondary Weapon, Armor, Accessory) and Skill Tomes.
② You can check the registered item for the exchange and the result item.
③ The types (A type/B type) of the exchanged Epic items can be changed by clicking the Type Change button.
④ You can check the required Gold or the grade and number of required Special Exchange Tickets for the Special Exchange.
- Required Gold or the grade and number of required Special Exchange Tickets change depending on the grade and tier of the registered exchange item.
- Special Exchange Ticket will be shown and used first and Gold as secondary if you have the required Special Exchange Ticket in your bag.
※ Please be aware that the Special Exchange Tickets stored in your Warehouse do not get counted.
