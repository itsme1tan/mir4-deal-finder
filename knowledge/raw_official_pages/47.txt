Party
In Mir 4, you can form parties with other players. Utilizing everyone's character classes well can form effective combat combinations.
Create Party
Recruit Member
Create Party
- You can create a party by tapping the Create Party button on the left of the main screen or clicking the 'Create Raid' button in the feature you want to play.
① You can have up to 5 players in a party.
② You can set your party to be a Public, Private, or a Clan Party. A private party requires entering a password to join the party.
③ You can restrict the Power Score of players who join the party.
Recruit Member
You can filter the list of parties by their purpose, and choose a party you want to join.
