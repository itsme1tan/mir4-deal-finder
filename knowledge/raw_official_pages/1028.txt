Domination Server
- Domination Server is consisting of 4 field areas and 2 exclusive contents.
Field : [Domination]Sabuk Castle, [Domination] Sabuk Province, Domination Labyrinth, and Black Dragon Valley
Exclusive Content : Tower of Black Dragon, Altar of Black Dragon
Requirements
1. Available hours
Round: 3 rounds per day in total.
- Each round will maintain for 4 hours, and players will be automatically expelled from Domination Server after the remaining time hits zero
Period: 3 rounds per day equals to 1 period.
- You can enter 1 round only during the Period.

※ All available hours will be based on regional server times.
※ Re-access to the Domination server can be freely conducted if more than 10 minutes are remaining.
※ If you quit the game while entering Domination Server, you will return to the Character Selection screen.
2. Character
- Domination server will become available after reaching level 90.
- You must fulfill all requirements for Domination Server.
- You will be resurrected from Sabuk Castle upon death.
- You can create a party regardless of each member's home server in Domination Server.
※ Domination server will be accessible whether you have joined clan or not.
Preparation
- How to access: Choose Menu > Transfer > Domination Server or click the 'Move server' button in Global Map.
① You can check your current Server, Available hours, and Domination server to enter.
② You can view all requirements to enter into the Domination Server.
③ If meeting all requirements, the Domination Server button will be activated, and you can enter into the server by consuming the Domination Server access count.
※ You can enter the server once per Period, and Domination server will be re-accessible during the round you have entered.
■ Domination Server exclusive content
Content Name
Explanation
Tower of Black Dragon
Mysterious tower in Domination server including powerful monsters, plenty of rewards, and various events that are waiting for Dragonians.
Altar of Black Dragon
A War-based content competing to occupy the Altar of Black Dragon located in Domination Labyrinth, Tower of Black Dragon, and Black Dragon Valley in Domination Server.
- Some content and systems that can be used on the normal server will be deactivated in Domination Server.
[Deactivated Contents and Systems]
Deactivated Contents: Hidden Valley, Magic Square, Secret Peak, World Boss, Clan, Valley Capture, Heist, Bicheon Castle Siege, Clan Expedition/Challenge, Expedition, etc
Deactivated Systems: Event, Territory Management, Castle Warehouse of home server, PvP Log, Bounty, Wanted List, Market, Server Transfer, Class Change, etc
PK rules in Domination Server
PK rules
- All characters in Domination Server will be regarded as Expedition members, and Expedition PK rules will be applied.
- 5 Lifes will be given upon participation as same as Expedition, and the Life will be shared with Expedition Content.
- 1 Life will be recovered per 1 Life of Water, and recovery will not be applied under MAX status.
- When all of your Lifes have been deducted upon death, you will be resurrected from [Domination] Sabuk Castle and cannot move out from the Castle.
- If being resurrected by resurrection skills of Spirit/Taoist after getting killed by PK, you can get resurrected from the area where you got killed with no loss of Life.
※ The death penalty from PK includes Internal injuries, EXP reduction, and item loss.
