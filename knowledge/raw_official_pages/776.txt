Special Boss
Special Boss is a weekly boss that spawns on every server on Monday / Friday at 22:00, which can be participated by all players of the Expedition members and the Natives.

Basics and Preparations
✔ Spawn Area
- [Crimson Emperor Utukan] : Spawns in 'Crimson Abaddon' which can be entered through the portal from Snake Valley 4F.
- [Demon Spider of Hell Krukan] : Spawns in 'Shackling Abaddon' which can be entered through the portal from Bicheon Valley 4F.
✔ Spawn Time
- [Crimson Emperor Utukan] : Spawns in every Friday, 22:00
- [Demon Spider of Hell Krukan] : Spawns in every Monday, 22:00
※ Special Boss spawns based on regional server times.
✔ Conditions and Participants
Precedent Quest
[Crimson Emperor Utukan] : Main Quest 'Poison Witch and Princess Cheonpa'
[Demon Spider of Hell Krukan] Main Quest 'Mastermind Behind Kidnapping, Join Yonggol'
Participants
Target : Natives and Expedition Members
Maximum number of participants : 450
Important Reminder
- Fast Travel to 'Crimson Abaddon / Shackling Abaddon' is not supported. Players must move to the portal in Snake Valley / Bicheon Valley to enter.
- Maximum of 450 players can enter the boss area and the players who have come after that will be enlisted in the waiting queue.
- Performing PK is possible and the alignment point will not decrease. Instead, the death penalty will still apply. (Loss of EXP, Loss of items, Occurance of Internal Injuries, Extended Revival Cool-downs)
-Special Boss spawns in every server.
How to participate
- Raid the special boss that spawns every Monday / Friday at 22:00. Players can defeat the special boss in their own servers and move to the other server to defeat the Special Boss spawned in the target server by using expedition.
- A spawn message will appear on the center of the screen when the Special Boss spawns.
Raid Rewards
Reward boxes can be obtained after defeating the Special Boss, including Mission rewards.
