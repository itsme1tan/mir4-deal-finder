You can earn bonus rewards by completing daily tasks and achievements.
Daily Task
Achievement
Daily Task
- Tap Task > Daily Task tab in the Menu.
- You can check the status of active daily tasks and claim rewards for completing each category once.
- Additional rewards are available for completing daily tasks 5 times and 10 times, which can be claimed by tapping the treasure chest button at the top of the screen.
Achievement
- Tap Task > Achievement tab in the Menu.
- Achievements are divided into Adventures, Upgrades, and Challenges, and you can view the completion requirements and rewards.
- Rewards can be claimed once an achievement is complete.
- Tap the 'Claim All' button at the top right to claim rewards for all completed achievements.
