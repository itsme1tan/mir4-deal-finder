Hall of Dragonians
Hall of Dragonians is a place where Dragonians can cast their votes to decide who truly deserves the title of supreme master of the Mir Continent, chosen from among the strongest Dragonians who have proven their skills.

Hall of Dragonians Basic Information
1. Candidate Selection: Every Monday, the top 5 users in the overall Power Score ranking are selected as 'Hall of Dragonians' voting candidates.
2. Participation Conditions: All Dragonians Level 10 or higher can cast a 'Like' vote for one of the voting candidates once per day.
3. Voting Process: Voting runs for one week, and Dragonians who participate in voting are immediately given a special buff reward.
4. Final Selection: When the voting period ends, the single Dragonian who received the most 'Like' votes is given the Most Votes reward.
Hall of Dragonians Reset Time
1. Hall of Dragonians Progress and Voting Period
Based on every Monday 02:00 (UTC+8), the Hall of Dragonians candidates and rankings are reset, and a new vote begins for one week immediately after the reset.
[Reset Time by Region (Weekly)]
- ASIA: Monday 02:00
- INMENA: Monday 00:00
- EU: Sunday 20:00
- SA: Sunday 15:00
- NA: Sunday 14:00
2. 'Like' Vote Count Reset Time
Every day, the number of available 'Like' votes (once per day) resets at the times specified below.
[Reset Time by Region (Daily)]
- ASIA: 02:00
- INMENA: 00:00
- EU: 20:00
- SA: 15:00
- NA: 14:00
Voting Method
1. Enter the Hall of Dragonians tab in [Menu > Ranking].
2. You can check the overall Power Score ranking or participate in voting by pressing the 'Like' button for one of the 5 Dragonians registered as Hall of Dragonians candidates.
