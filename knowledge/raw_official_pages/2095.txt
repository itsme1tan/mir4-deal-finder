Scripture Hall
Scripture Hall is a special archive in the Mir Continent that stores Peerless Skills. In this content, you can complete the forms of the archived skills to gain additional stats and exclusive rewards.

Scripture Hall
- Scripture Hall unlocks once your character reaches Level 120.
- Navigate to [Menu > Codex > Scripture Hall] to access the Scripture Hall window.
① You can view the total Power Score gained from the Scripture Hall at the top left of the screen.
- Click the magnifying glass button on the right to view the stat bonuses obtained through the Scripture Hall.
② The magnifying glass icon on the right also provides a summary of relevant Scripture Hall details.
③ The Scripture Hall is composed of the 1st Scripture Hall, 2nd Scripture Hall, and the Restoration Center.
- 1st Scripture Hall / 2nd Scripture Hall: Register various Skill Tomes and Arcane Tomes to earn stat bonuses and rewards.
- Restoration Center: Restore Skill Arcane Tomes and Ancient Arcane Tomes using Burnt Skill Tomes.
④ Use the list on the left to select the Peerless Skill you want to register.
⑤ Select a Form to restore within that skill.
- Each Peerless Skill contains up to 10 Forms, and completing each Form grants unique stat bonuses and rewards.
⑥ Check the required materials for each Form and the rewards/stats granted upon completion.
- Click the [Reward] button on the right to manually receive the completed Form’s reward.
⑦ You can filter Forms by progress, acquired stats, or required materials within the selected Peerless Skill.
⑧ View the total stats gained from completed Forms and utilize the various functions at the bottom.
- Auto-Register: Automatically registers required materials for the selected Form.
- Progress: View the number of Forms completed or currently in progress.
Restoration Center
- Through the Restoration Center, you can use Burnt Skill Tomes to obtain Skill Arcane Tomes and Ancient Arcane Tomes.
※ Burnt Skill Tomes can be obtained from the ‘Redmoon Purgatory’ in the Spiritual Center Area.
[Game Encyclopedia - Redmoon Purgatory Shortcut]
① Select the Arcane Tome you wish to restore from the right side of the screen.
② Check the materials required and the resulting item for the restoration.
- Restoring an Arcane Tome requires a matching-grade Burnt Skill Tome, Purified Soul Stone, and Energy.

Crafting/Exchange Info
- At the NPC <Palace Craftsman> Hyo Janggyeong in Bicheon Castle, Sabuk Castle, and [Domination] Sabuk Castle, you can craft higher-grade Burnt Skill Tomes or exchange Skill / Ancient Arcane Tomes for other items.
