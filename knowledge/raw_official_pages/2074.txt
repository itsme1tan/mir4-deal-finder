Conquest Treasure
Conquest Treasures are a new type of equipment that can be equipped in the Sanctuary of Hydra. The Sanctuary of Hydra absorbs the energy contained within the Conquest Treasures to generate special daily rewards. The stronger the equipped Conquest Treasures, the greater the rewards produced.
[Conquest Treasure Basic Information]
1. Up to 3 Conquest Treasures can be equipped in [Conquest - Sanctuary of Hydra] and higher-grade Conquest Treasure yield greater rewards.
2. Contribution rewards are distributed daily at 00:00 (UTC+8) based on the currently equipped Conquest Treasures. Unclaimed rewards will not accumulate and will be lost.
3. Equipped Conquest Treasures can be unequipped at any time.
4. Conquest Treasure and reward distribution follow the same schedule as the EXDRA Season.

[How to Obtain Conquest Treasure]
1. Purchase of limited-time products via WEMIX PAY
2. Trading via the XDRACO website
- All Conquest Treasures come with the XDRACO attribute.
3. Trading via the in-game Unified Market
[Conquest Treasure Promotion]
1. Conquest Treasures can be promoted to a higher grade through the Promote system.
2. Only Conquest Treasures of Rare to Epic grade are eligible for promotion.
3. Promotion requires 4 Conquest Treasures of the same grade as materials.
4. On a Great Success, the promoted Conquest Treasure will gain a special XDRACO attribute.
5. On failure, the first registered material will be returned, while the remaining materials and resources will be consumed.
[Conquest Treasure Promotion Process]
1. Access the Promotion Menu
1) [Conquest > Conquest Treasure Equip Slot above Sanctuary of Hydra > Promote Conquest Treasure]
2) [Equipment > Select the Conquest Treasure to Promote > Promote]
2. Select the Conquest Treasure to Promote, check the required materials, and proceed with the promotion attempt.
※ When a Conquest Treasure is selected, three materials of the same grade will be automatically selected. If the promotion fails, the selected Conquest Treasure will be retained, but the other three materials will be consumed.
