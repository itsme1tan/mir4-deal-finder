Mythic Equipment
Mythic Equipment represents the pinnacle of power, accessible only to Dragonians who have transcended even the greatest masters of the Mir continent. Unlock abilities beyond imagination, far surpassing everything that came before it.

Mythic Material Information
Mythic NPC
- You can obtain items related to Mythic Equipment through the Spiritual Center and Arcadia Spirit Village NPCs.
1) Spiritual Center NPC <Mythic Dragon Material> Martial World Master: Mythic Equipment and Mythic Dragon Artifacts
2) Arcadia Spirit Village NPC <Mythic Spirit> Diviner Octy: Mythic Spirits
① You can obtain items related to Mythic Equipment and Mythic Dragon Artifacts through the Spiritual Center NPC <Mythic Dragon Material> Martial World Master.
- Awaken: A feature that allows you to awaken Legendary equipment (Tier 4) and Legendary Dragon Artifacts (Black Dragon or White Dragon Artifact) that have been fully enhanced to Stage 15 into Mythic grade.
- Invoke: A function that allows you to combine 3 Legendary Dragon Materials to obtain the 'Divine Dragon's Soul', one of the main materials for Awakening.
② Mythic Spirits can be obtained through the Arcadia Spirit Village NPC <Mythic Spirit> Diviner Octy.
- Invoke: Allows the combining of 3 Legendary Spirit Stones to obtain a Mythic Spirit Stone.
Equipment Awaken
① You can select the Legendary equipment or Legendary Dragon Artifact to awaken from the list on the right side of the screen.
- Equipment Awakening can be performed with Legendary equipment (Tier 4) or Dragon Artifacts (Black Dragon or White Dragon Artifact) that have been fully enhanced to Stage 15.
② On the left side of the screen, you can check the registered equipment, the materials required for Awakening, and the stats that change upon Awakening.
- All equipment awakened to Mythic grade will become non-tradable.
- When Awakening equipment, its Enhancement Stage and Dragon Forge Stage will be retained, but all additional options applied through Enchant will be reset.
- Awakening equipment improves its base options, increases its maximum Enhancement Stage to 20, and grants a special unique effect exclusive to Mythic Equipment.
- Mythic Equipment will not be destroyed upon Enhancement Failure, but its Enhancement Stage may decrease.
- Mythic Dragon Artifacts cannot be used as growth materials for Dragon Artifact Benediction.
③ Tap the [Awaken] button to awaken the registered equipment to Mythic Equipment.
④ You can view the unique effect and stats of the Mythic Equipment once Awakening is complete.
- The unique effect of Mythic Equipment differs depending on the equipment type.
- Each type of equipment has 10 possible unique effects, and one of them is randomly applied.
- The applied unique effect can be changed using the 'Manifest Ability' feature.
Equipment Invoke
① You can select the materials to use for Invoke from the list on the right side of the screen.
- Equipment Invoke is a feature that allows you to combine 3 Legendary Dragon Materials to obtain a 'Divine Dragon's Soul'.
② You can view the materials registered for Invoke, and proceed with Invoke by clicking the [Invoke] button.
③ You can check the success rate of Equipment Invoke and the items obtainable upon success or failure.
- If Invoke is successful, you will obtain a 'Divine Dragon's Soul' and your current Divine Dragon's Promise Points will be reset to 0.
- If Invoke fails, you will receive 1 random Legendary Dragon Material and accumulate 1 Divine Dragon's Promise Point.
④ Equipment Invoke includes the Divine Dragon's Promise system, where you can check your current Promise Points and perform a Guaranteed Summon.
- Each time you accumulate 5 Divine Dragon's Promise Points, the number of available Guaranteed Summons increases.
- When performing a Guaranteed Summon, you will obtain a 'Divine Dragon's Soul' and the available Guaranteed Summon count will decrease.
※ 'Divine Dragon's Soul' can also be obtained, at a low probability, through Disenchanting a Rare Stage 3 or higher, or by opening a Mythic Ancient Box.
Manifest Ability
① There are two ways to access the Manifest Ability screen.
- In-Game Menu (+) > Character > Equipment > Mythic Equipment > Manifest Ability Button
- In-Game Menu (+) > Forge  > Enchant > Manifest Ability Tab > Select Equipment > Manifest Ability Button
② You can view the unique effects available through Manifest Ability.
③ You can check the required materials or proceed with Manifest Ability from this section.
- Required materials for Manifest Ability: Stone of Manifest Ability x1, Energy x20,000, Copper x50,000
④ After performing Manifest Ability, you can decide whether to apply the new effect from the right side of the screen.
- Clicking the [Apply] button will apply the newly selected effect through Manifest Ability.
- Clicking the [Cancel] button will retain the previously owned effect.

Spirit Invoke
① You can select the Spirit Stones to use for Invoke from the list on the right side of the screen.
- Spirit Invoke is a feature that allows you to combine 3 Legendary Spirit Stones to obtain a 'Mythic Spirit Stone'.
② You can view the registered materials for Invoke and proceed with Invoke by clicking the [Invoke] button.
③ You can check the success rate of Spirit Invoke and the items obtainable upon success or failure.
- If Invoke is successful, you will obtain 1 random 'Mythic Spirit Stone', and your current Divine Dragon's Promise Points will be reset to 0.
- If Invoke fails, you will receive 1 Spirit Stone that does not have a Party Leader Skill, and 1 Divine Dragon's Promise Point will be accumulated.
④ Spirit Invoke includes the Divine Dragon's Promise system, where you can check your current Promise Points or perform a Guaranteed Summon.
- Each time you accumulate 15 Divine Dragon's Promise Points, the number of available Guaranteed Summons increases.
- When performing a Guaranteed Summon, you will obtain 1 random 'Mythic Spirit Stone', and the number of available Guaranteed Summons will decrease.

- Can be obtained at a certain probability as a reward from clearing Hell Raid or Clan Expedition, or from Disenchanting Uncommon Stage 2 or higher items.

- Can be obtained at a certain probability by opening Epic/Legendary/Mythic Ancient Boxes that appear on the Domination Server.
