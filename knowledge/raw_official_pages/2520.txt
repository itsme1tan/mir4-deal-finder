Divine Manifestation Chart
Divine Manifestation Chart is a mysterious painting that captures the manifestation (現身) of a great god. Each time you piece together the scattered Divine Pages to restore the painting, additional stats are granted, and upon reaching certain Restoration stages, you can acquire a unique passive skill effect.

Divine Manifestation Chart
- The Divine Manifestation Chart is content that unlocks once a character reaches Level 80.
- You can move to the Divine Manifestation Chart screen through [Menu > Conquests > Divine Manifestation Chart].
① Select the Divine Manifestation Chart you wish to restore on the left/right side of the screen.
- Restorable Divine Manifestation Charts are divided into a total of 4 types (Hwayang / Haryeon / Yuwoon / Gangrim).
- The Restoration stages of the Divine Pages are [Common → Rare (32) → Epic (8) → Legendary (2)], and each Divine Manifestation Chart contains a total of 42 Divine Pages.
② The Power Score gained through the Divine Manifestation Chart is shown at the top left of the screen. Tap it to view the detailed stats and passive skill effects applied through the Divine Manifestation Chart.
③ Summary information about the Divine Manifestation Chart is shown at the top right of the screen.
- Tap it to view detailed information on the Restoration materials of the Divine Pages that make up the Divine Manifestation Chart and the stats obtainable upon Restoration.
Scripture Page Restoration
- Through Scripture Page Restoration, you can raise the Restoration stage of the Divine Pages that make up a Divine Manifestation Chart.
※ The Restoration stages of the Divine Pages consist of [Common → Rare (32) → Epic (8) → Legendary (2)].
① Select a Divine Page to restore within a Divine Manifestation Chart to view the required materials, success rate, stats gained, and more.
- The Restoration success rate and the amount of materials consumed vary depending on the type of stats gained upon Restoration.
Scripture Page Bonding
- Each time you restore 4 adjacent Divine Pages, Scripture Page Bonding occurs, and slots where Scripture Page Bonding has taken place can advance to the next stage of Scripture Page Restoration.

Divine Manifestation Chart Passive Effects
- When you restore every Divine Page of a single grade within a Divine Manifestation Chart, you gain that painting's unique passive effect.
- As the Restoration grade climbs to higher stages ([Rare → Epic → Legendary]), the power of the passive skill increases, granting even stronger effects.
